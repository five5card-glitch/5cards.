Frequently Asked Questions

How does the heatless curler work?
Simply wrap sections of your slightly damp or dry hair around the silk rod before bed. The rod holds your hair in a curl shape overnight. In the morning, unwrap and you'll have beautiful, bouncy curls — no heat, no damage!
Does it work on all hair types?
Yes! LunaCurl works on all hair types — straight, wavy, curly, coily, short, long, thin, or thick. The key is adjusting how you wrap and how damp your hair is. Our video tutorial covers every hair type.
How long do the curls last?
Most customers report curls lasting all day (8-12 hours). For extra hold, use on slightly damp hair with a light mousse or setting spray before wrapping. Results get better as your hair gets used to the routine!
